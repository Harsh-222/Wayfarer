Important:
- Copy your existing backend/.env into the final project. Do not upload it to GitHub.
- Required backend variables: PORT, MONGODB_URI, JWT_SECRET, CLIENT_URL, ADMIN_EMAIL, ADMIN_PASSWORD.
- MongoDB Atlas must allow your current IP for local development.
- Real destination photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Internet is required for those photos.
- The local SVG fallback keeps cards usable if a remote photo fails.
